Research themes

Statistical foundations
for reliable AI

How can statistical reasoning make AI-assisted scientific findings more reliable, interpretable, and reproducible? The conference brings together researchers in statistical theory, machine learning, and applied science to examine that question.

Reliable inference

Quantify uncertainty and assess calibration and robustness when AI systems learn from adaptive or dependent data, or face shifts in the populations they serve.

Complex and structured data

Study how representations, distances, and sampling designs affect learning from trajectories, images, networks, and other structured observations.

AI-assisted discovery

Validate AI-generated hypotheses, assess causal claims, and test whether findings reproduce across populations and settings.

Ways to participate

The meeting is designed for researchers across academia, industry, and government, with opportunities for graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, and junior faculty to present work and get feedback.

Tuesday, May 4

Present a poster

A planned open call will invite posters, with particular encouragement for early-career researchers. Presenters are expected to receive feedback from faculty or senior researchers.

Poster selection criteria

Planned review criteria include scientific contribution, methodological soundness, and clarity of communication. Submission instructions and deadlines are forthcoming.

May 3–5

Join a data challenge

Teams will tackle mentored challenges in AI for fintech and AI for biomedical research. The organizers plan to announce the datasets and scientific questions before the meeting.

What teams will submit

Teams are expected to submit executable code and a short report covering their design, assumptions, uncertainty, failure modes, and use of AI. Evaluation will consider reproducibility and interpretation alongside performance.

Participant support. The organizers are seeking funding to make the conference more accessible to early-career researchers. Availability, eligibility, and application details are pending.
